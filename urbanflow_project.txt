UrbanFlow – Smart Mobility System for the GTA

Project Overview

UrbanFlow is a smart mobility platform designed to solve everyday
commuting problems in the Greater Toronto Area (GTA).

Drivers, cyclists, and pedestrians face daily issues such as: -
Dangerous intersections - Lack of parking information - Unsafe bike
routes - Inefficient routes due to construction - Rising fuel costs

UrbanFlow integrates crowdsourced data, city datasets, and predictive
models to provide safer and more efficient navigation.

Feature 1: Dangerous Intersection Reporting

Problem: Many intersections experience near‑miss accidents that never
get formally recorded. City planners often react only after crashes
occur.

Solution: A crowdsourced reporting system that identifies high‑risk
intersections before accidents happen.

Features: - One‑tap near miss reporting - Add details such as cyclist
conflict, pedestrian conflict, or aggressive driver - Data clustering to
identify hotspots - Risk heatmap of intersections

Data Sources: - Open city accident datasets - User reports - Traffic
volume datasets

Output: Map showing: - High‑risk intersections - Medium‑risk
intersections - Safer intersections

Feature 2: Smart Street Parking Map

Problem: Drivers in dense urban areas waste significant time searching
for parking. This increases congestion and emissions.

Solution: A live parking availability map using crowdsourced reports and
predictive modeling.

Features: - Users mark when they leave a parking spot - Users mark when
they find an empty spot - Prediction model estimates probability of free
parking per street segment - Integration with parking restrictions such
as permits, street cleaning, and time limits - Navigation to the closest
likely parking spot

Feature 3: Bike Route Safety Index

Problem: Cyclists struggle to find safe routes, especially where bike
lanes disappear.

Solution: A route safety scoring system.

Safety Score Factors: - Bike lanes (high importance) - Traffic speed
(high importance) - Accident history (medium) - Lighting (medium) - Road
width (low)

Features: - Safest bike route option - Accident history visualization -
Bike lane coverage map - Safety score per street

Feature 4: Smart Eco Route Planner

Problem: Drivers face unpredictable costs due to construction detours,
traffic congestion, fluctuating gas prices, and different vehicle fuel
efficiencies.

Solution: UrbanFlow generates cost‑optimized routes instead of only
fastest routes.

Inputs: - Vehicle model - Fuel efficiency - Fuel type - Trip destination

Integrated Data: - Traffic conditions - Road construction zones -
Predicted gas prices - Terrain and distance

Route Optimization Formula: Total Cost = Fuel Cost + Time Cost + Risk
Cost

Fuel Cost depends on: - distance - vehicle fuel efficiency - predicted
gas price

Time Cost depends on: - traffic congestion - construction delays

Risk Cost depends on: - dangerous intersections - road accident history

Route Output Options: - Fastest Route - Cheapest Route - Safest Route

System Architecture

Frontend: - React - Mapbox or Leaflet - Tailwind UI

Backend: - Node.js - Express API

Database: - PostgreSQL with PostGIS - Redis caching

Data Processing: - Python microservices

Example Scenario

A driver travels from Mississauga to Downtown Toronto.

UrbanFlow: 1. Detects construction zones 2. Predicts gas prices 3.
Calculates fuel cost for the specific vehicle 4. Avoids dangerous
intersections 5. Suggests optimal routes

Outputs: - Route A: Fastest - Route B: Cheapest - Route C: Safest
